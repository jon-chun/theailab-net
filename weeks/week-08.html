EXTRA CREDIT (optional): Mini-Project #2plus

Once your MP2 resubmission is in, you can keep building for extra credit. Mini-Project #2plus, assigned Sunday, Oct 11 after the first MP2 final submissions were graded, is an optional menu of fifteen independent exercises for your own CMS, each taking 15 to 60 minutes and worth 5 to 10 points according to its time and difficulty, added to your MP2 grade (up to +30 in all). Pick any that interest you, in any order, and stop whenever you like. The menu is built from the parts of MP2 that were introduced but not required, or scored only lightly: nine features (media uploads with alt text, categories and tags, revision history, scheduled publishing, site search, moderated comments, a plugin system, switchable themes, and a production configuration that refuses unsafe settings), four workflow skills (a pull-request cycle with review, a /prototype side session, a decisions table with an ADR, and a usage-budget re-forecast from your ledger), and two security hardenings (a router-generated access-control test, and a login that neither reveals which emails exist nor allows unlimited password guesses). Every exercise has an acceptance checklist and a scoring line you can grade yourself before you submit, and MP2plus must not break MP2: the MP2 checker and test suite may show no failure that was absent at your mp2plus-base tag. Start by tagging your current MP2 commit mp2plus-base, read the MP2plus manual and the MP2plus rubric in the starter repo, and submit by tagging mp2plus-final and emailing the one-page report by Tuesday, Oct 20, 2:40 pm (no-penalty grace until Wednesday, Oct 21, 2:40 pm).

Correction posted Oct 12: earlier that day this page gave Thursday, Oct 22 as the MP2plus deadline. The deadline is Tuesday, Oct 20, 2:40 pm, with no-penalty grace until Wednesday, Oct 21, 2:40 pm. The points were also revised on Oct 12: each exercise is now worth 5 to 10 points, up to +30 in all (the earlier text said 2 to 4 points, up to +15), and the manual and rubric are at version v3.
