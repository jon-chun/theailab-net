EXTRA CREDIT (optional): Mini-Project #2plus

Once your MP2 resubmission is in, you can keep building for extra credit. Mini-Project #2plus is an optional menu of fifteen independent components for your own CMS, each estimated at one to two hours, each worth 2 to 4 points that are added to your MP2 score (up to +15 in all). Pick any that interest you, in any order, and stop whenever you like. The menu is built from the parts of MP2 that were introduced but never required or scored: nine features from the stretch menu (media uploads with alt text, categories and tags, revision history, scheduled publishing, site search, moderated comments, a plugin system, switchable themes, and a live admin deploy behind a security gate), four workflow skills (a pull-request cycle with review, a /prototype side session, a decisions table with an ADR, and a usage-budget re-forecast from your ledger), and two security hardenings (a router-generated access-control test, and a login that neither reveals which emails exist nor allows unlimited password guesses). Every component has an acceptance checklist you can grade yourself before you submit, and MP2plus must not break MP2: the MP2 checker and test suite still have to pass. Start by tagging your current MP2 commit mp2plus-base, read the MP2plus manual and the MP2plus rubric in the starter repo, and submit by tagging mp2plus-final and emailing the one-page report by Thursday, Oct 22, 2:40 pm (no-penalty grace until Friday, Oct 23, 2:40 pm).
