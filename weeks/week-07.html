Introduction

This week, between Mini-Projects #2 and #3, affords us the opportunity to reinforce aspects of the mattpocock skills SWE toolkit, learn more canonical development documents, and generalize various approaches to AI Software Engineering. Along the way, we see how AI agents, harnesses, and automated SWE/SDLC lead to AI software factories, and how they fundamentally redefine, and potentially overwhelm, the role humans may play in our brave new world.
