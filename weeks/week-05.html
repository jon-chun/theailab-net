Introduction

With a firm foundation in Claude Code CLI, skills, prompting, and using them for webdev we expand our scope to encompass Software Engineering (SWE), the Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC), and various related concepts like spec-driven development (SDD) and test-driven development (TDD). SWE and SDLC bring a discipline upon which robust, scalable, and flexible knowledge workflows can be built beyond just for software. This week will prepare you for Mini-Project #2: Designing a Web Content Management System (CMS) due week 6.
